# Report template

Use this structure. Keep it scannable. Every conclusion names its source (e.g. "Auto policy form, Part D, p. 7" or "Chase Guide to Benefits, Auto Rental CDW, published version at <url>").

---

## Are you fully covered? **YES** / **NO** / **CAN'T CONFIRM YET**

One or two sentences: the bottom line and the biggest gap (if any).

### Rental
- Location, dates/length, vehicle type
- Renter on the agreement: [name], paying with [card] ([primary cardholder / AU])
- Additional drivers on the agreement: [names]

### Coverage by driver

| Driver | Damage (full value) | Loss of use & fees | Liability ≥ $300k | PIP (if required) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Renter] | ✅ Auto policy (primary, $500 deductible) + card picks up deductible | ✅ Card | ✅ $500k; recommend $1M+ | ✅ out-of-state clause | **Pass** |
| [Friend] | ❌ Not on renter's policy; own policy unverified | ⚠️ Unverified | ❌ $100k | ⚠️ | **Fail** |

Legend: ✅ covered · ❌ gap · ⚠️ unverified (document not provided or unclear)

### Gaps and why
Numbered list. For each: driver, coverage, why it fails, source.

### Fix it for this trip (short term)
Specific to each gap, e.g.:
- Buy the rental company's **CDW/LDW** for the trip (note: this voids card coverage, so use it only when the card doesn't help)
- Buy **SLI/LIS** (state the quoted limit)
- Third-party rental damage coverage or a short-term non-owner liability policy
- Change who rents / who drives / which card pays (e.g. the spouse-as-AU fix)
- Remove a driver who can't be covered

### Fix it for good (long term)
- Raise auto liability limits (e.g. to 250/500/100 or higher)
- Buy a **true umbrella** of $1M+ (especially if assets exceed $300k)
- Add collision/comprehensive or a rental endorsement to the auto policy
- Get a card with rental coverage that includes loss of use and additional drivers (or primary coverage)
- Add a spouse as an authorized user
- A **non-owner auto policy** for anyone who rents but doesn't own a car

### Before you drive
- **Never let anyone not on the rental agreement drive.** If they do, there is no damage coverage.
- Decline the CDW/LDW **only** if relying on card coverage, and pay the **entire** rental with that card.
- **Credit cards never cover liability.**
- Take photos/video of the car at pickup and return.
- After any incident: police report, photos, notify the insurer and card benefit administrator promptly, and request the fleet utilization log for any loss-of-use claim.

### Still to confirm
Items marked ⚠️, with what document or phone call would resolve each.
