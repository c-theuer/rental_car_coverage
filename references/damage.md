# Damage coverage (the rental car itself)

Damage = collision + comprehensive (theft, vandalism, weather, glass) to the rental car, plus the extra charges rental companies bill after a loss.

## Order of payment (default)

1. **Personal auto policy** (primary). Applies only if the policy carries collision and/or comprehensive. Liability-only policies give **no** damage coverage on a rental.
2. **Credit card rental benefit** (usually secondary). Pays what the personal policy doesn't: the deductible, and often loss of use, admin fees, and towing.
3. **Rental company CDW/LDW** (a waiver, not insurance). If bought, it replaces both of the above. Buying it usually **voids** the card benefit.

If the driver has **no** personal auto insurance, a secondary card pays first anyway (see "No personal auto policy" below). Some cards state **primary** coverage (they pay first even when the driver has a personal policy, which isn't involved). Always report what the guide says. A primary card is still bounded by its own maximum and exclusions.

## Personal auto policy: what to check

| Item | Where to find it | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Collision / comprehensive on the policy | Declarations | Present on at least one vehicle? Deductibles? |
| Rental covered at all | Policy form, Part D "non-owned auto" / "temporary substitute" definitions | Standard ISO wording covers a private passenger auto, pickup, or van **not owned by or furnished for regular use** of you or a family member. Long-term rentals and some vehicle types may fall outside. |
| Who is covered | Definitions of "you" and "family member" | "You" = named insured **and spouse living in the household**. "Family member" = relative living in the household. See `household.md`. |
| Limit on a rental | Policy form, Part D limit of liability; any endorsement | ISO default: actual cash value or cost of repair. Many insurers cap the rental at the value of, or "broadest coverage on any," covered vehicle. |
| **Multiple cars** | Policy form, "other insurance" / non-owned auto provisions | Check whether the rental gets the **broadest** coverage among insured cars (common), the coverage of a particular car, or something else. If one car has collision and another is liability-only, confirm the rental inherits the collision coverage. Report the effective limit and deductible. |
| Loss of use | Policy form, Part D "transportation expenses" or loss-of-use clause | ISO PAP pays loss-of-use charges on a non-owned auto under "transportation expenses," capped per day and in total. The cap depends on the form edition: $20/day and $600 max in older editions, $30/day and $900 max reported for the 2018 edition (PP 00 01 09 18), and higher if the declarations or an endorsement say so. Many insurers' own forms cap it lower or exclude it. Rarely covers admin fees or diminished value. |
| Territory | Policy form, "policy territory" | ISO: US, its territories and possessions, Puerto Rico, Canada. Not Mexico or overseas. |
| Exclusions | Part D exclusions | Business use, ride-share, trucks over a weight limit, racing, unlisted driver. |

**Value check:** if the rental is worth more than what the policy will pay (e.g. a luxury car rented by someone whose policy caps at their own older car), the difference is a **damage gap**.

## Credit card benefit: what to check

Pull these from the Guide to Benefits (auto rental collision damage waiver section):

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Primary or secondary | And in which countries. Some cards are primary abroad and secondary at home. **New York residents:** state law makes many otherwise-primary card benefits (e.g. Chase Sapphire) secondary for NY residents. Ask the user's home state. |
| Eligible renter | Usually "cardholder" (sometimes including authorized users). **The primary driver (the renter named on the agreement) must be the primary cardholder or an authorized user on the card, paying with their own card.** |
| Authorized drivers | Usually anyone listed on the rental agreement. Some guides limit it. |
| Payment condition | The **entire** rental must be paid with the card (or that card's points). Split payment can void coverage. |
| Decline CDW/LDW | Required. Accepting the company's waiver usually voids the card benefit. |
| Maximum | e.g. "up to the actual cash value" or a dollar cap such as $75,000. Compare to the rental's value. |
| Loss of use | Covered? Usually requires a **fleet utilization log** from the rental company. |
| Admin fees, diminished value | Covered? |
| Towing / storage | Covered? To the nearest qualified repair facility? |
| Rental length | Commonly 15 consecutive days in the home country and 31 days abroad. Longer rentals may be excluded entirely. |
| Excluded vehicles | Commonly: expensive/exotic/antique cars, trucks, cargo vans, vans with 9+ seats, motorcycles, RVs, some SUVs. |
| Excluded countries | Commonly Israel, Jamaica, Republic of Ireland, Northern Ireland. Verify. |
| Claim deadlines and documents | Notice within N days; documents required. |

If you looked the guide up online, cite the URL and the date shown on the guide, and mark the card findings "confirm against your own guide."

## Combining them (per driver)

For each driver on the agreement:

- **Covered by personal policy?** (yes / no / unverified) + limit + deductible
- **Covered by card?** (yes / no / unverified) + max + which fees
- **Result:**
  - Full value covered? Deductible picked up by the card?
  - Loss of use, admin fees, diminished value, and towing covered by at least one source?
  - Any remaining gap in dollars or category

## Common gaps

- **Friend driving, renter's policy.** The renter's policy usually does **not** cover an unrelated friend. The friend may be covered by **their own** policy's non-owned auto coverage if they're on the agreement (verify in their policy), and by the card as an authorized driver (verify in the guide). If neither applies, the friend fails the damage test.
- **No personal auto policy.** A secondary card automatically acts as **primary** when the driver has no personal auto insurance or other insurance covering the loss. Standard Visa Signature guides, for example, say "If You do not have personal automobile insurance or any other insurance, this coverage acts as primary," and then pay the damage plus loss-of-use and admin charges. Confirm the wording in the user's guide, but **don't** recommend switching to a primary card on this basis. If no card benefit either, the user needs the counter's CDW/LDW or third-party rental damage coverage.
- **Loss of use not covered by either.** Common with personal policies alone. A card that covers loss of use closes it.
- **Excluded vehicle or country, or rental too long.** The card provides nothing. Fall back to the personal policy (if the territory applies) or buy the counter's waiver.
