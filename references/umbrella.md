# Umbrella insurance

## True umbrella vs follow-form excess

| | True (personal) umbrella | Follow-form excess |
|---|---|---|
| What it covers | Liability above underlying policies **and** some claims those policies don't cover | **Only** what the underlying policy covers, above its limit |
| Territory | Usually **worldwide** | Same as the underlying (for auto: US, territories, Canada) |
| Extra coverages | Often personal injury: **libel, slander**, defamation, false arrest, invasion of privacy | None beyond the underlying |
| When underlying doesn't apply | **Drops down** above a self-insured retention (SIR, e.g. $250–$1,000), subject to the "retained limit" wording | Pays nothing |
| Rental car abroad | Can cover liability for a rental overseas (check exclusions and retained limit) | Usually nothing |

### How to tell them apart in the policy
- Look for a **coverage territory** clause that says "anywhere in the world."
- Look for **personal injury** in the definitions (libel, slander, defamation).
- Look for a **self-insured retention / retained limit** that applies when no underlying insurance covers the loss. That is the drop-down.
- Phrases like "follows form," "subject to the same terms, conditions and exclusions as the underlying insurance," or "excess liability policy" signal follow-form.

### Watch out for
- **Required underlying limits.** Most umbrellas require the user to maintain specific auto limits (e.g. 250/500/100). If underlying coverage is lower or doesn't apply (abroad), the umbrella may pay only **above** the required underlying amount, leaving that layer to the user. Read the "retained limit" definition.
- **Exclusions** for vehicles rented for long periods, business use, or vehicles used for hire.
- Whether **all household drivers** are insureds (usually resident relatives yes; friends no).

## Recommendation rules

- Assets over **$300k** and no umbrella → recommend a **true** umbrella of at least **$1M** (more if assets are higher). Typical cost is a few hundred dollars a year for $1M, but tell the user to get quotes.
- Has only a follow-form excess policy and travels abroad → explain the gap and recommend a true umbrella.
- Assets under $300k → still note the $1M liability recommendation; an umbrella is optional but inexpensive protection for future earnings.
- An umbrella usually requires higher underlying auto limits. Raising them is often part of the long-term fix.
